Overview

A campus built around the working day.

The College's facilities are not amenities; they are working rooms, each aligned to a specific part of the Cambridge curriculum or the rhythm of a student's day. Practicals are sat in the rooms they are rehearsed in. Lessons take place in classrooms equipped for the way they are taught.

01

Individual Workstations

Every laboratory and IT room is sized so each student works at their own bench or terminal, with no shared stations during practicals.

02

Cambridge Aligned

Apparatus lists, bench layouts and timing mirror Cambridge IGCSE and A Level practical requirements, so the examination room is familiar.

03

Safety First

PPE, eye-wash and emergency equipment in every wet-lab; firewall-filtered browsing in the IT suite; supervised access throughout the school day.

04

Modern Equipment

Digital balances, data-logging probes, smart boards, fibre internet and audio-visual integration: equipment students will meet again at university.

Facility · I

Chemistry Laboratory

A fully equipped, state-of-the-art Chemistry laboratory built and resourced to Cambridge International Education standards. Individual benches allow every student to run their own experiment, with no rotating and no observing from the back of the room.

Capability

A bench for every student, not a station for every pair.

Cambridge science is taught hands-on. Our Chemistry lab is sized so each student has their own bench position, their own apparatus, and their own measurements to take, every practical, every lesson.

  • Individual workstation for every student during practical sessions
  • Full Cambridge apparatus list: IGCSE 0620 and A Level 9701 coverage
  • Safety equipment & PPE at every bench: goggles, gloves, lab coats
  • Separate, locked chemical store with COSHH-compliant reagent shelving
  • Gas, water and electrical service to every bench position
  • Analytical balances, pH meters and volumetric glassware to Class A tolerance
Facility · II

Physics Laboratory

A dedicated Physics laboratory equipped for mechanics, electricity, optics and data-logging, built to the same Cambridge International compliance standards as Chemistry, with individual benches so every student runs every experiment.

Capability

Mechanics, electricity, optics, fully resourced.

Equipment is held in matched class sets so every student in the room can work the same experiment at the same time. Apparatus is audited at the start of each academic year against the Cambridge published list.

  • Individual workstation for every student during practical sessions
  • Full Cambridge apparatus list: IGCSE 0625 and A Level 9702 coverage
  • Safety equipment & PPE for high-voltage, laser and radiation work
  • Mechanics kits: light gates, ticker-timers, pulleys, masses, springs
  • Electricity benches: multimeters, signal generators, oscilloscopes
  • Optics rails, ray boxes, lenses, diffraction gratings; data-logging probes
Facility · III

College Library

A quiet, well-stocked reading and study space at the centre of the academic day. The collection covers the full Cambridge syllabus, with reference material, a lending section, and dedicated zones for both solo and collaborative study.

Collection & Study

A reading room, not a holding pen.

The library is staffed during the academic day and zoned so silent individual study and supervised group-work can run side by side without interference. The full collection is searchable from any terminal.

  • Quiet study area with individual reading tables and task lighting
  • Reference and lending collection covering the Cambridge syllabus
  • Dedicated group-work zone for collaborative project sessions
  • Digital catalogue with search terminals on the library floor
  • Periodicals, atlases and exam-board past-paper archive
  • Staffed librarian desk during academic hours
Facility · IV

IT Laboratory

A modern computing suite supporting Cambridge ICT and Computer Science coursework, with a workstation for every student, fibre internet and firewall-filtered browsing for safe online learning.

Computing & Connectivity

Modern computers, filtered fibre.

Every student logs in to their own workstation, on a fibre-backed internet connection that is filtered at the firewall, so research, coursework and online assessment all happen on the same supervised network.

  • Modern desktop computers, dedicated workstation per student
  • Fibre internet uplink for the whole campus
  • Firewall-protected safe browsing, filtered for school use
  • Audio-visual facilities: projector, classroom audio and display switching
  • Wired and Wi-Fi access to printers, scanners and shared storage
  • Supervised access during ICT and Computer Science lessons
Facility · V

College Auditorium

A 250-seat auditorium that hosts the College's calendar of events: academic assemblies, performances, guest lectures, drama productions, seminars and the annual prize-giving.

Events & Performance

Seating for up to 250.

The auditorium is the College's main shared room, sized to hold the whole upper school in a single sitting, equipped for both spoken-word and performance events.

  • Seating for up to 250 attendees
  • Stage with lectern and event-grade lighting
  • House audio system with wired and wireless microphones
  • Hosts academic assemblies, prize-givings and guest lectures
  • Hosts drama productions, music recitals and dance performances
  • Seminar and external-examination configurations as required
Facility · VI

Smart Classrooms

Classrooms equipped with smart boards and integrated audio-visual systems, so lessons can move fluently between written work, projected materials, video and the digital curriculum.

Teaching Technology

Smart boards and audio-visual integration.

Each smart classroom is built around a touch-enabled board and an integrated AV system, so teachers can switch between annotation, projection, video and digital coursework without leaving the front of the room.

  • Interactive smart boards in every equipped classroom
  • Integrated audio-visual systems: display, speakers, input switching
  • Touch annotation over digital teaching materials
  • Connectivity to Cambridge digital resources and lesson archives
Facility · VII

College Cafeteria

The cafeteria is the social centre of the school day, a clean, supervised space for breaks, lunch and informal study, located on the main campus.

Break & Lunch

Service Hours

Runs through morning break and the full lunch hour every school day, providing a predictable rhythm to the student timetable.

Clean & Safe

Maintained Space

Routine cleaning between sittings and hand-wash stations throughout the service area keep the cafeteria to a high hygiene standard.

Supervised

Staff Oversight

Teaching staff supervise the cafeteria during all service hours, maintaining the same ordered environment as the classroom.

Daily Life

A clean, supervised space to break.

The cafeteria runs through morning break and the lunch hour, with staff supervision throughout. Seating is arranged to support both mealtimes and informal study between lessons.

  • Indoor seating with capacity for break and lunch service
  • Service counter for daily meals and snack items
  • Hand-wash stations and routine cleaning between sittings
  • Supervised by staff during all service hours
