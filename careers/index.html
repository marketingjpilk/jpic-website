Why Join Us

A Place to Grow
As an Educator

We are an institution where teaching is a vocation, not a job description. Our faculty are mentors, scholars and lifelong learners, supported by a community that values professional development as much as academic rigour.

01 · Vocation

Purpose-Led Teaching

Work within a values-driven, Catholic educational framework that respects every child as an individual. Your teaching shapes character, not just grades.

02 · Curriculum

World-Class Cambridge Pathway

Teach the full Cambridge International curriculum, from Early Years through Advanced Level, with rich resources, faculty support and global recognition.

03 · Growth

Professional Development

Funded Cambridge teacher training, mentorship from senior faculty, leadership pathways, and time set aside each term for continued learning.

Application Process

How to Apply

STEP 01

Review the Role

Expand the position you are interested in and review the responsibilities, requirements and closing date in full.

STEP 02

Submit Application

Email your CV, a cover letter and the names of two referees to hr@jpeducation.net, quoting the role title in your subject line.

STEP 03

Interview & Demo

Shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview with the academic leadership team, plus a demonstration lesson for teaching roles.

STEP 04

Offer & Onboarding

Successful candidates receive a formal offer and join our Cambridge induction programme prior to the start of the academic term.
