Our Philosophy

Where the whole student
takes the field.

Sport at St. John Paul II International College is timetabled, coached and, where chosen, examined as a Cambridge qualification — including Cambridge IGCSE and AS & A Level Physical Education. Based in Wennappuwa, Sri Lanka, the college offers a sports programme that every learner from Primary through A Level participates in. The habits formed on a field or court — discipline, resilience, teamwork — are the same ones that define a person long after school ends. Those at representative level enter a year-round programme built around fitness, leadership and belonging to a squad.

Programme

A weekly training rhythm, year-round.

Every representative squad trains a minimum of three sessions per week, with strength and conditioning, skill work and match-play kept in separate blocks, timed to the tournament calendar. Formal partnerships with provincial associations mean our students train at accredited venues alongside club and provincial athletes. Physical Education is also available as a Cambridge IGCSE and AS & A Level examined subject, giving academically ambitious students a pathway to recognised qualifications in sport.

  • Three structured training sessions per week per primary squad
  • Inter-house tournaments each term to keep participation broad across year groups
  • Formal coaching partnerships with Western Province and national sports bodies
  • Cambridge IGCSE and AS & A Level Physical Education available as examined qualifications
Recognised Standards

Training environments that reflect our ambitions.

Our students train at association-accredited venues, under supervision that matches the standards of provincial development programmes. Every venue is vetted for safety and coaching quality, so parents can be confident their child is being developed, not simply kept active.

  • All training venues meet provincial association standards for the relevant sport
  • Coaching staff hold national or internationally recognised qualifications
  • Students competing at inter-school level are covered under the school's sports affiliation
  • Performance records maintained termly and shared with parents at review meetings
